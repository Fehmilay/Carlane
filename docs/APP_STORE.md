## Ratings and privacy

* **Age rating:** 9+ / USK 6. Infrequent cartoon violence: pixel cars explode in a stylised
  8-bit burst. No blood, no people, no real-world weapons aimed at people.
* **Data collection:** none. The game stores progress locally (Capacitor Preferences +
  `localStorage`). No analytics SDK, no tracking, no account. In-app purchases are processed by
  Apple; RevenueCat receives only the anonymous purchase receipt. Declare "Purchases" →
  "Not linked to you" and "Not used for tracking".
* **Export compliance:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` (set in `Info.plist`).
* **Offline:** the whole game runs offline. Only the store needs a connection.
